# Code of Conduct

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect confidentiality, licences, trademarks, and privacy.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Keep reviews focused on the change and its impact.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Submitting secrets, customer data, or restricted material.
- Plagiarism, misattributed vendor claims, or deliberate evidence manipulation.
- Spam, repeated off-topic promotion, or disruptive bad-faith participation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where practical, and avoid exposing sensitive reports publicly.
